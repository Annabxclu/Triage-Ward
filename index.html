Name your hospital

ER — OpenLabRadiologyCardiology+18 wards
  • You open with a free Emergency Room (one doctor, one nurse), plus a Pharmacy, Laboratory and Radiology with one staff member each. Every other department must be bought.
  • Walk-ins arrive at random: about 100 per game day to start, more as you accept new insurance plans. Each goes straight to the department for their condition.
  • Red patients have 1 real hour, yellow 2 hours, green 3 hours. Run tests, uncover findings, make the diagnosis, treat, then dispense meds.
  • Stuck, or the right ward is locked? Transfer them to another hospital.
  • Time runs as game time: 1 game minute is 15 real seconds, so a 10-minute CT head takes 2½ real minutes. The Speed button runs 2× or 4× faster. Closing the page pauses your hospital.
